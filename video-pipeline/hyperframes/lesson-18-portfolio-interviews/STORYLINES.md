# Lesson 18 — Portfolio, resume and AI interview prep: storylines

Source: lesson `portfolio-interviews` in `src/lib/modules/m6.ts`. Every claim below comes from that lesson. The
resume numbers (1,200 documents, 87% hit rate@4, 60 questions, ₹0.04 per query) are the lesson's own examples and
must be labelled illustrative on screen.

**The one idea:** a project only counts when it is proof: a demo, a diagram and measured numbers behind every line.
**Remembered as:** "Every number on my resume is printed by a script in my repo."

Assumptions for all three: 1920×1080, 90–120 s, ElevenLabs narration (same voice as lesson 1), no music,
BeyondPlaced look. Picked without waiting for the user, as the run brief asks: **A**.

## A. One resume line, rebuilt (recommended — the viewer watches a weak line become a strong one)

- **Device / form:** life before and after + the worked example; one frame that rebuilds.
- **What you see and feel:** Rahul's resume, with one line under Projects: "Worked on an AI chatbot". A recruiter's
  skim passes over it. The page then slides aside and the proof stack is built under that line, one layer per beat:
  a demo window with an answer streaming, an architecture strip from question to answer, a results table. The
  resume line rewrites itself from those layers. An interviewer's follow-up question appears, and the answer comes
  straight out of the results table.
- **First 2 seconds:** a white resume page with "Worked on an AI chatbot" under Projects, and a highlight bar
  sweeping past it without stopping.
- **Beats:** the weak line and the skim → four layers named → demo → architecture → metrics → the line rewrites:
  action, scope, metric (numbers labelled illustrative) → "How did you get 87%?" → "60 questions, script in the
  repo" → three pinned repos, not six → the one line.
- **Look:** the site's brand: mist ground, white paper and cards, deep teal structure, coral only on the key item of
  each scene (the weak line, the metric in the new line, the follow-up question), Inter + JetBrains Mono.
- **Sound:** narration only; warm and unhurried.

## B. The interview, played forward

- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day; a two-person exchange as chat bubbles.
- **What you see and feel:** an interview as a message thread. Each interviewer question lands, and Rahul's answer
  pulls up the artefact that backs it: the demo for "show me", the diagram for "how does it work", the table for
  "how well". Questions he cannot back with an artefact stay unanswered and grey.
- **First 2 seconds:** "Walk me through your project." already on screen and a timer starting at 2:00.
- **Beats:** walk me through → demo → how does it work → diagram → how well → metrics → how did you measure →
  script → the resume line these answers came from → the one line.
- **Look:** mist ground, teal interviewer bubbles, white answer cards, coral on the one unanswered question.
- **Sound:** narration plus a soft tick as each question lands.

## C. The placement notice board (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** something every student knows; an endless zoom.
- **What you see and feel:** a college notice board crowded with identical resumes. The camera dives into one that
  has a QR code, through the code into a live demo, through the demo into its diagram, through the diagram into the
  results table, and back out to the same resume, now the only one with a shortlist stamp.
- **First 2 seconds:** a cork board of near-identical resumes, one with a small QR code in the corner.
- **Beats:** the board → the QR → demo → diagram → metrics → back out to the resume line → the one line.
- **Look:** warm cork and paper in the brand palette, coral on the shortlist stamp only.
- **Sound:** narration with a paper rustle on each zoom.
